Some legitimate non-profit or foundation-based internships tie a portion of the intern's stipend to fundraising performance or donations raised, disclosed transparently in the offer letter, with no upfront payment demanded from the candidate. This compensation structure is unusual and worth noting to the candidate as a moderate-risk pattern worth understanding fully, but it is meaningfully less severe than a scam that demands direct payment from the candidate, uses high-pressure tactics, or hides behind a fake or unverifiable company identity. This pattern should generally be scored as Medium risk, not High, when the organization has verifiable legitimacy and no direct payment request exists.
